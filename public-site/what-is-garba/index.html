GARBA OF GUJARAT

What is Garba?

Garba is a devotional circle-dance tradition from Gujarat, performed especially during Navratri. Dancers move around a lit garbha-deep or an image of Amba while singing, clapping and instruments carry the rhythm.

01 · GARBA

A devotional circle dance from Gujarat.

Garba is closely associated with Navratri and the worship of feminine energy, or Shakti. UNESCO describes the dance as taking place around a perforated earthenware pot lit with an oil lamp, or around an image of Amba.

Singing, clapping and instrumental accompaniment help hold the group rhythm while dancers move together around the centre.

02 · GARBA AND DANDIYA RAAS

Related, but not the same dance.

Garba centres on collective circular movement, traditionally around the garbha-deep or Amba, and often uses clapping as part of the movement. Dandiya is commonly used for forms of Raas in which dancers use small wooden sticks as part of the rhythm and choreography.

Gujarat Tourism presents Garba and Dandiya/Raas alongside one another during Navratri while keeping the forms distinct. People may move between them on the same festival night as the music and dance change.

03 · NAVRATRI MUSIC

The music follows the dance.

Navratri gatherings in Gujarat can move through sung Garba, instrumental accompaniment and Raas/Dandiya music. The sound changes with the form, but the music supports collective movement rather than sitting apart from it.

04 · SANEDO

A Gujarati narrative folk-song form.

Sanedo is a Gujarati narrative folk-song form, especially associated with North Gujarat. Gujarati Vishwakosh describes it as a humorous, dramatic narrative-song tradition with distinctive stories and folk tunes.

The name is traced to the Sanskrit word sneh, meaning affection. PlayGarba keeps Sanedo as its own listening category rather than treating it as another name for Garba.

Garba of Gujarat was inscribed in 2023.

UNESCO inscribed Garba of Gujarat on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2023.
