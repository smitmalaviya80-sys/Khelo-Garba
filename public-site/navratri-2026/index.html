01 · DATES

When is Navratri 2026?

Incredible India's national Navratri listing gives Sunday, 11 October through Monday, 19 October 2026 for the nine-night festival.

Gujarat Tourism's state-sponsored Vibrant Navratri Festival in Ahmedabad is separately listed from 11 through 20 October 2026. PlayGarba keeps those two official date ranges separate rather than treating them as the same event.

03 · VIBRANT NAVRATRI

Vibrant Navratri Festival 2026: GMDC Ground, Ahmedabad.

Incredible India lists the Gujarat Tourism-hosted Vibrant Navratri Festival at GMDC Ground in Ahmedabad from 11 to 20 October 2026. Its current listing includes nightly Maha Aarti, cultural programming, a handicraft bazaar and an opening-night performance involving more than 1,000 artists.

Event programmes can change. Check the official tourism listing before making travel or attendance plans.

What are the Navratri 2026 dates?

Where is Gujarat's Vibrant Navratri Festival 2026?

Where can I listen to Gujarati Garba online?

PlayGarba lets you explore Gujarati Garba by Traditional, Dandiya, Devotional, Folk, Sanedo, Fusion and Nonstop listening contexts, with YouTube used for executable playback.

What is Nonstop Garba on PlayGarba?

Nonstop is the long-form listening area for a full continuous Garba recording or set. When a source publishes verified chapter starts, PlayGarba can use those chapters for navigation inside the same recording rather than treating each chapter as a different recording. Open Nonstop Garba.

How do I choose Garba music for a Garba night?

Choose by the kind of dancing and listening you need, not by an invented ranking. Start with Traditional for core Garba, Dandiya for Raas-led listening, or Devotional for worship-centred listening. Use Nonstop when you want one longer continuous recording, then explore Folk, Sanedo or Fusion when those sounds fit your gathering.

What Garba styles are represented in PlayGarba?

The main PlayGarba listening categories are Traditional, Dandiya, Devotional, Folk, Sanedo and Fusion. Nonstop is a separate long-form listening area. These are catalogue and listening routes, not claims that every Gujarati folk or Navratri recording belongs to the same tradition.

What is the difference between Garba and Dandiya?

Garba and Dandiya Raas are related Gujarati dance traditions often seen during the same Navratri celebrations, but they are not the same form. Start with What is Garba? for the broader Garba context.
