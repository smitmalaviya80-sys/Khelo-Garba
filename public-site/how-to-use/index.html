How to use PlayGarba.

Open the player, choose a Garba style and press play. Explore and Nonstop are there when you need them.

Three things to know.

The player is designed to stay understandable without a tutorial.

01 · OPEN

Go to the player.

Use playgarba.com for listening.

02 · CHOOSE

Pick a Garba style.

Traditional, Dandiya, Devotional, Folk, Sanedo or Fusion.

03 · PLAY

Play or explore.

Stay with the current music, search the catalogue or open Nonstop.

Use the live address for listening.

03 · EXPLORE

Search when you know what you want.

Explore opens the catalogue. Search by song or artist, then use the catalogue views to move through releases and Garba categories.

Open Explore ↗

04 · CONTROLS

The important controls stay close to the music.

Use play and pause, previous and next, favourites, share and queue where those controls are available for the current listening state.

Play / pauseStart or pause the current track.
Previous / nextMove through the active listening context.
FavouriteSave a track for later.
QueueSee what is coming next.

05 · NONSTOP

Use Nonstop for longer sets.

Nonstop is for continuous Garba releases and longer performances. It uses the same player, so you can move in and out without learning another interface.

Open Nonstop ↗

06 · YOUTUBE

Playback comes from YouTube.

PlayGarba uses verified YouTube routes for playback. The video stays collapsed unless you choose to open it. If a catalogue item does not yet have a verified YouTube route, it can remain unavailable until that route is added.
