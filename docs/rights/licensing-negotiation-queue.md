# Licensing negotiation queue

Status: prepared, not contacted, 2026-09-07

Purpose: convert the label-level acquisition analysis into a repeatable business-development queue. This document does not authorise outreach and does not record any granted rights.

## Common first-call package

Every serious catalogue conversation should request the same minimum data before GARBA discusses ingesting files:

1. Current legal name of the entity that can license the relevant sound recordings.
2. Confirmation that the contact is authorised to negotiate/license those masters.
3. Machine-readable catalogue export, preferably CSV/XLSX/JSON, with:
   - ISRC;
   - recording title and version;
   - primary and featured artists;
   - release title;
   - original release date;
   - duration;
   - language/genre when available;
   - © owner;
   - ℗ owner;
   - current digital licensor/distributor;
   - territories controlled;
   - any excluded platforms/uses.
4. Garba/Raas/Folk/Devotional subset indicator.
5. Whether the catalogue can be licensed for user-selected interactive streaming from GARBA-controlled storage/CDN.
6. Whether GARBA may receive WAV/FLAC masters and transcode them into web-streaming formats.
7. Whether ordinary browser/CDN buffering and caching is permitted.
8. Whether user-initiated offline playback is permitted and, if so, under what controls.
9. Artwork/metadata use rights.
10. Reporting requirements and whether ISRC-level usage reports are required.
11. Commercial structure for 100-track, 250-track, 500-track and larger catalogue scenarios.
12. Separate identification of underlying musical/lyric rights that the master licensor does not control.

## Queue 2: Soor Mandir

Current catalogue signal: 245 rows labelled `Soor Mandir`, plus 15 rows separately labelled `Soor Mandir / SM Digital`.

Objective: identify the present legal licensor and determine whether the historic/current Soor Mandir catalogue can be licensed as one digital package.

Context to resolve on the first call:
- the Soor Mandir brand and verified publishing channels are active;
- IPRS material lists Soor Mandir as an owner/publisher;
- historic filed contract schedules show Soor Mandir entered digital-music/streaming licences;
- the old `Soor Mandir Music Private Limited` corporate record is struck off.

First ask therefore begins with identity, not price:
1. Who is the current legal owner/licensor of the Soor Mandir master catalogue?
2. What is the relationship between `Soor Mandir`, `SM Digital`, and `Soor Mandir / SM Digital`?
3. Which entity should execute a new interactive-streaming licence?
4. Can they provide a current rights catalogue with ISRC and ©/℗ fields?

Only after that identity gate passes should GARBA request 100/250/whole-Garba-catalogue pricing.

## Commercial comparison table to maintain

For every proposal record:
- licensor;
- catalogue size offered;
- GARBA overlap count;
- approved new-to-GARBA recordings;
- fixed fee;
- minimum guarantee;
- revenue share;
- per-stream royalty if any;
- reporting cadence;
- territory;
- term;
- renewal;
- master delivery included;
- artwork included;
- underlying works included/excluded;
- offline included/excluded;
- takedown/termination terms;
- projected cost per usable track.

No commercial proposal should be selected solely on headline catalogue size. Rank proposals by usable rights-clean Garba tracks, catalogue quality, rights scope, operational simplicity and total cost.
