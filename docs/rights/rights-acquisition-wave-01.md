# Rights acquisition wave 1

Status: active research, 2026-09-07

Target: first 100 rights-cleared direct masters toward the 700-track hosting goal.

## Priority logic

The catalogue report currently shows the largest uncleared artist-credit batches as Rutvi Pandya (64), Hemant Chauhan (55), Atul Purohit (50), Geeta Rabari (47), Hardik Dave + Rahul Munjariya (44), Aditya Gadhvi (37), Aishwarya Majmudar (33), Falguni Pathak + Tushaar Trivedi (30), Pamela Jain (29), and Pamela Jain + Kirtidan Gadhvi (28).

Do not interpret an artist-credit batch as one legal rights-owner batch. Recording ownership is release-specific and sometimes label-specific. The first job is therefore to split artist-credit clusters by master owner.

Next action: contact Rutvi Pandya/management seeking a catalogue-level digital streaming and redistribution grant, approved lossless masters, territory/term, commercial-use permission, artwork permission, and explicit offline-cache position. Start with Ramzat 1.0, 2.0, and 3.0 as one requested bundle.

## First-100 strategy

Best current path is not simply the top three artists by count. The acquisition order should favour owner concentration:

1. Rutvi Pandya Ramzat releases.
2. Hardik Dave + Rahul Munjariya `Garbi` / `Garbi 2` through Sur Sagar.
3. Atul Purohit releases controlled by Rutumbhara Entertainment.
4. Geeta Rabari Official-owned releases.
5. Aditya Gadhvi's 2245114 Records DK subset if control is confirmed.
6. T-Series Hemant Chauhan subset only after commercial terms are known.

The first four lanes alone are large enough that two or three successful catalogue-level agreements could plausibly produce the first 100 direct masters.

## Required grant terms

Every agreement must explicitly address:
- right to host and stream the sound recording directly from GARBA infrastructure;
- commercial/non-commercial scope;
- territories;
- licence term and renewal/termination;
- whether GARBA may transcode supplied masters to web formats;
- whether users may temporarily cache for ordinary streaming;
- whether explicit offline downloads are permitted;
- artwork/cover permission if used;
- attribution requirements;
- takedown process;
- whether underlying musical and literary works are included or require separate licensing;
- confirmation that the licensor has authority to grant the rights.

No track moves to `cleared` until the licence evidence and approved master are both in hand and the track has a rights-gated direct-audio entry.
