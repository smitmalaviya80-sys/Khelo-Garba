# Sanedo discovery: YouTube-only search

Checked: 26 September 2026

## Current Garba repository inventory

The current generated `data/songs.json` has **1,706 song records** and **4 records categorized Sanedo**. Those four are from one 2007 release, *Sanedo Sanedo*, by Achal Maheta, Sargam Vyash, Ansh Maheta, Shilpa Aiyyar, Piyush Parmar & Pratiksha Desai. They are `Rang Pichkari`, `Poonam Ni Raat`, `Ashmani Rang Ni Chundani` and `Jai Jai Aarasur Rani`. All four have unknown duration, no YouTube ID and no bundled audio. The repository currently classifies these as metadata-only.

## What the YouTube results establish

- The current repository has four Sanedo records, all metadata-only from one release.
- Direct YouTube searches and playlist inspection surface **at least 26 individual-song/version/medley candidates** and **18 distinct playlist records or playlist leads** above. These are discovery candidates, not 26 verified unique recordings: some rows are alternate uploads, lyric videos, multiple editions of one set, dance clips or uncertain matches. The playlist section intentionally includes clearly marked mixed and dance-only playlists because they are useful discovery sources, but they should not be counted as songs.
- YouTube search itself also returns dance Shorts, tutorials, generic Garba sets and titles that only mention Sanedo. Those are excluded from the song count unless they point to a clearly titled music recording.
- YouTube results expose substantially more than the four current catalog rows. However, this pass does **not** establish hundreds of unique Sanedo songs. A responsible “hundreds” count needs deeper YouTube result pagination and systematic deduplication of songs, versions and mix chapters. Search-result quantity or playlist size alone cannot establish unique, importable tracks.
