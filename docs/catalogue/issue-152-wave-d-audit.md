# Issue #152 Catalogue Wave D audit

Date: 2026-09-08

Issue: #152, modern/popular Garba catalogue completeness for Falguni Pathak, Kirtidan Gadhvi, Geeta Rabari and Aditya Gadhvi.

## Scope and safety rules

This wave adds high-value, provider-backed catalogue material without treating compilation reuse, spelling variants or random concert uploads as new canonical recordings.

Playback routing follows the repository hierarchy:

1. authorised direct audio already in the repository
2. exact official YouTube video or provider-published chapter
3. exact Amazon Music track
4. exact Apple Music song
5. exact Spotify track
6. conservative verified release fallback

No YouTube or streaming audio is downloaded or extracted. No timestamp is inferred. No provider identifier is constructed from neighbouring tracks. Release pages are not described as exact-song routes.

## Remaining work before issue #152 can be closed

The quantitative target is met, but the issue also asks for an exhaustive four-artist audit. The following legitimate catalogue areas still need a dedicated evidence pass:

- Falguni Pathak: older non-stop and dandiya masters, with recording-level duplicate comparison against later reissues and compilations
- Kirtidan Gadhvi: remaining Rangili Ramzat, Tahukar and Nortani Raat editions
- Geeta Rabari: Zankaar 3, Taal sequels, Khamma 2 and other major provider-backed Garba releases
- Aditya Gadhvi: Amber Gaje and a final taxonomy-fit review of major popular releases and collaborations

Issue #152 should remain open until those areas have been reconciled or explicitly rejected with evidence.
