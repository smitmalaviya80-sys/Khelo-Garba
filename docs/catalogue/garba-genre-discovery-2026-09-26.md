# Garba genre discovery: YouTube songs and playlists

Checked: 26 September 2026

This is a separate genre discovery list from [Sanedo discovery](sanedo-youtube-discovery-2026-09-26.md). It follows the repository genres in `data/taxonomy.json` and gathers YouTube recordings, singer-led channel collections, and playlists that can expand each lane. Google was used to find/index the YouTube pages; all playback links below go to YouTube or YouTube Music. No songs were played.

## What is in this pass

This first pass covers **Traditional Garba / Raas**, **Raas–Dandiya**, **Mataji / Devotional**, **Krishna Garba / Raas**, and **Bollywood / Filmi Garba**. The taxonomy also has rhythm/style tags such as Tran Taali, Be Taali, Hinch, Dodhiyu, and Dakla; it has not been possible to classify every recording into those substyles from search metadata alone. Search titles and playlist descriptions are evidence of association, not a claim that every song fits only one dance form.

For *Ranglo*, YouTube provides separately linked song clips (often about one to three minutes) from a 35-track traditional collection. Those short excerpts are track-level recordings but may be less complete than the album versions. Long videos explicitly titled as non-stop, jukebox, album stream, or full-album stream are kept as collections, not counted as single songs.

## Genre expansion queue
