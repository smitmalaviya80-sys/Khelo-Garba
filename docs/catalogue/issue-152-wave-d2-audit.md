# Issue #152 Catalogue Wave D2 audit

Date: 2026-09-08

Issue: #152, follow-up catalogue completeness for Geeta Rabari and Kirtidan Gadhvi.

## Outcome

Wave D2 adds two complete split-track releases already identified as high-value gaps in the Wave D audit:

- Geeta Rabari — `Zankaar 3.0` (2024), 28 canonical tracks
- Kirtidan Gadhvi — `Tahukar 10` (2022), 13 canonical tracks

This adds 41 canonical songs, 2 canonical releases and 39 exact official YouTube chapter routes. It also adds the official continuous sets to the Nonstop browser while keeping the continuous masters distinct from the split-track catalogue editions.

No audio is downloaded or redistributed. No timestamps are inferred.

## Geeta Rabari — Zankaar 3.0

The canonical split provider edition contains 28 tracks and totals 63:35. Geeta Rabari's official YouTube upload publishes a chapter list for the continuous performance.

Twenty-six chapter routes are exact and title-aligned.

Two tracks deliberately remain on provider fallback:

- track 11: the split provider album lists `Chare Dishano Pavagadh`, but the official YouTube chapter at 23:15 is titled `Unjah Betha Che Umiya Maa`
- track 12: `Tame Avjo Re Ambaji Dhame` is present in the official chapter list, but its timestamp is malformed in the published description

The mismatch is preserved rather than guessed around. No neighbouring timestamp or track-number shift is used.

A separate continuous `Zankaar 3.0` provider edition runs 63:04. It remains a Nonstop listening master and is not collapsed into the 28-track split release.

## Kirtidan Gadhvi — Tahukar 10

The canonical provider album contains 13 tracks. Kirtidan Gadhvi Official's YouTube upload publishes all 13 track titles and chapter starts in matching order.

All 13 canonical tracks therefore receive exact official YouTube chapter routes.

Amazon and Qobuz expose small one-second duration-rounding differences on some split tracks. Canonical song durations follow the Amazon display values and the release note preserves the provider variance.

The separate continuous `Tahukar 10 (Nonstop Garba)` master runs 30:47 and remains a distinct Nonstop listening entry.
