## Research still open

This index is a set of focused release reports, not a claim that all remaining missing individual YouTube routes were found. High-impact unresolved areas include the 2016 Non Stop Garba release (12/12 individual rows missing; a two-hour RDC full-set lead is not edition/tracklist-verified), 2017 Ramzat (14 rows now audited; candidates still need a final expanded-description review), the 2025 Rankar release (22 unresolved), and edition-conflicted older albums. The attached snapshot also omits or misstates current targets such as the 19-song Rangili Ramzat 7 album, so check the live catalogue before mapping its rows. Rangoli Vol. 16 has a focused report with two title discrepancies requiring resolution. The separate 1–105 batch audit also leaves explicit open candidates and historical sets; it is not a fresh verification of every row in those batches. One additional exact route has been verified for Batch 59's Dwarika No Nath single; it remains a research result pending any separately authorized catalogue implementation.
