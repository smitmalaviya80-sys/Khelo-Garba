## 1. Genre Distinction & Musical Identity

### 1.1 What is Raas & Dandiya?
Dandiya Raas (દાંડિયા રાસ) is the ancient stick dance rooted in the pastoral tradition of Lord Krishna and the Gopis of Vrindavan. Performed with polished wooden sticks (*dandiyas*), it is an energetic, rhythmic, geometric dance where partners face each other in concentric rotating circles and strike sticks in complex, synchronized choreography.

### 1.2 Structure & Rhythm
- **The Clacking Percussion:** The physical acoustic strike of the polished hardwood sticks provides a secondary percussion layer directly on top of the dholak, drums, and synthesizers.
- **Musical Meter:** Strict 4/4 or 6/8 meter. Steady, driving, unbroken tempo allowing seamless partner switches.
- **Choreography:**
  - Double concentric circles rotating in opposite directions.
  - Basic 4-beat strike: Strike partner's right stick, left stick, spin, strike the next partner in the advancing circle.
  - Advanced variations: *Chokadi* (four-corner strike), kneeling strikes, overhead spins, and jumping strikes.

### 1.3 Invariants: What belongs here vs. elsewhere
- **MUST have:** Strict steady driving tempo arranged for paired stick striking.
- **DO NOT mix with:**
  - Clapping hands without sticks (`tran-taali`, `be-taali`).
  - Solo/freestyle couplets (`sanedo`).
  - Syncopated diagonal stepping without sticks (`dodhiyu`).
