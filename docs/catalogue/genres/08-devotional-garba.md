## 1. Genre Distinction & Musical Identity

### 1.1 What is Devotional Garba?
Garba is at its foundation an act of sacred worship (*Bhakti*). Devotional Garba divides into two spiritual pillars:

#### A. Mataji / Shaktipith Devotion (`mataji-devotional`)
- Dedicated to the Mother Goddess (*Adhya Shakti*, *Amba*, *Bahuchar*, *Kalika*, *Khodiyar*, *Chamunda*, *Meldi*, *Mogal*, *Ashapura*).
- Includes the foundational nightly rituals:
  - **Aarti:** *Jai Adhya Shakti* (sung synchronously across Gujarat at the end of each Navratri night).
  - **Stuti & Chhand:** *Vishwambhari Akhil Vishwa Tani Janeta*, *Aanand No Garbo*.
  - **Thal & Garbi:** Sacred food offerings and devotional praise.

#### B. Krishna Garba / Raas (`krishna-garba`)
- Dedicated to the playful romance and cosmic dance of **Lord Krishna**, **Radha**, and the **Gopis** of Gokul, Mathura, and Dwarka.
- Lyrical themes: Flute (*bansuri*), stealing butter (*makhan chor*), Yamuna riverbanks, and moonlit Raas.
- Artists: Atul Purohit, Hemant Chauhan, Kirtidan Gadhvi, Purushottam Upadhyay.

### 1.2 Invariants: What belongs here vs. elsewhere
- **MUST have:** Explicit devotional, stuti, aarti, or sacred leela lyrics directed to the Divine Mother or Krishna.
- **DO NOT mix with:**
  - Secular party or film remixes (`dj-remix`, `bollywood-filmi`).
  - Humorous festival banter (`sanedo`).
  - Electronic trap beats without devotional gravity (`electronic-fusion`).
