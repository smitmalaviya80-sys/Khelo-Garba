## 1. Genre Distinction & Musical Identity

### 1.1 What is Fusion & Modern Garba?
Modern Garba represents the dynamic evolution of traditional Gujarati folk rhythms meeting 21st-century global production:
- **Electronic / Fusion:** Synthesis of traditional folk vocals and dholak with synthesizers, sub-bass, trap percussion, EDM risers, and ambient soundscapes (*Coke Studio Bharat*, Bandish Projekt, Sachin-Jigar).
- **DJ / Remix:** Non-stop club and party edits designed for wedding sangeets, club nights, and modern youth festival grounds.
- **Hip-Hop Garba:** Fusion of Gujarati folk couplets with Gujarati rap, hip-hop breakbeats, and urban youth street culture.
- **Bollywood / Filmi Garba:** High-budget orchestral, cinematic Garba compositions produced for major motion pictures (Sanjay Leela Bhansali, Sachin-Jigar, A.R. Rahman, Pritam, Amit Trivedi).

### 1.2 Invariants: What belongs here vs. elsewhere
- **MUST have:** Modern synthesized production, electronic rhythm sections, hip-hop/rap verses, or film soundtrack provenance.
- **DO NOT mix with:**
  - Pure acoustic temple or courtyard garba (`traditional-garba`, `tran-taali`).
  - Traditional Kathiyawadi Dayro folk singing (`folk-lokgeet`).
  - Pure Sanedo folk couplets without electronic/rock fusion (`sanedo`).
