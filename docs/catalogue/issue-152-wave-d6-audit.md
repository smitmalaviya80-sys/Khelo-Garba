- 14 canonical songs
- 1 canonical split-track release
- 14 exact Amazon Music track routes

## Split and continuous editions

Amazon Music publishes the split `Nortani Raat (Garba)` edition on August 29, 2025 with 14 tracks. Its displayed durations sum to 33:10.

Amazon Music and Kirtidan Gadhvi Official separately publish a one-track `Nortani Raat (Non Stop Garba)` master at the same 33:10 duration. The split album is the canonical song edition. The continuous master is retained as one Nonstop listening entry and is not converted into guessed YouTube chapters.

This avoids double-counting the same programme as two separate 14-track releases.

## Exact playback

Every canonical song uses the exact href exposed by Amazon's official 14-track album page. Several destination pages were not cached by the crawler, but their exact provider URLs were still exposed by the album's song links. Those hrefs are evidence; no adjacent or pattern-derived identifiers are used.

The official continuous YouTube master is `bHW9wnLkhp8`. It remains a full-set route only.

## Duplicate and credit handling

All 14 songs preserve the joint credit `Kirtidan Gadhvi & Anita Pandit`.

Some titles, including `Jilan Jilva Gya Ta`, also occur in older Kirtidan releases. Wave D6 does not collapse the 2025 joint-artist edition into the older recording because the provider publishes it as part of a distinct 2025 programme with different primary credits.
